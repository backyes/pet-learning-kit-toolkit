## What is deliberately absent

The same toolkit was originally built against a chapter of a contemporary novel. That chapter, its Chinese translation, and its narration audio are **copyrighted** and are **not** included here. They live in a separate private repository for personal study only.

If you fork this project, keep it that way: generated kits for copyrighted books are for private use, not for publication.

## Audio generation

All audio in this repository was produced locally with the macOS `say` command (voice *Daniel*, en-GB) and encoded with `ffmpeg`. No commercial audiobook recording was used.
